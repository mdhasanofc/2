Quick answers

Questions people ask before requesting a quote.

Keep technical detail available but secondary. The primary action remains a fast quote request.

What happens when it is cloudy or at night?

Solar output changes with available sunlight. At night, panels are not generating, so your home draws from a battery if available or from the grid in a grid-connected system.

Do I need a battery with solar?

No. A panel-only system can still be useful. A battery can store excess solar energy for later use, so the best option depends on your usage pattern, goals and budget.

How do you decide what size system I need?

System design should consider your energy profile together with roof space, orientation, pitch, shading and what you want to achieve by going solar.

Can my system still use the electricity grid?

Yes. Many residential solar systems remain grid-connected. Your home can use solar when available and draw additional energy from the grid when required.
